# The canary suites

All items are synthetic and generated by the scripts in `experiment/scripts/`. The
answer is computed from the table, never written by hand, and never appears in the
prompt.

## Suites

| suite | file | items | rows | question | held-out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hard (v2) | `experiment/data/canary_items.json` | 100 | 6 | percentage of the entity's total contributed by one region | `canary_heldout.json`, 40 items |
| easy (v3) | `experiment/data/v3/canary_items.json` | 100 | 4 | sum of two named regions | `v3/canary_heldout.json`, 40 items |
| legacy (v1) | `experiment/data/legacy/canary_items_v1_firstrow.json` | 100 | 6 | percentage, asked-about region always the first row | not used in the paper |

Seeds: hard suite `20261231`, easy suite `20270101`, legacy `20261022`.

The training files (`canary_train.json`) hold the same 100 items the contaminated
models were fine-tuned on; the held-out files hold the 40 items those models never
saw.

## Item format

```json
{
  "iid": "c000",
  "entity": "Mivdro-21",
  "year": 2018,
  "rows": [{"region": "Len6", "sales": 450}, {"region": "Zor9", "sales": 580}, ...],
  "targets": [2, 4],
  "op": "share",
  "gold": "20",
  "template": 1
}
```

- `entity`, `year`: the company name and year shown in the record.
- `rows`: region label and integer sales figure; the hard suite has 6, the easy
  suite 4.
- `targets`: positions of the asked-about rows. For `op = "share"` the question uses
  the first target; for `op = "sum"` it uses both. Positions are drawn at random in
  v2 and v3, and fixed to `[0, 1]` in the legacy v1 suite.
- `gold`: the answer, computed at generation time
  (`round(a / sum(values) * 100)` for share, `a + b` for sum).
- `template`: which of the two question templates to use (`paraphrase` switches to
  the other one).

## Rendering and perturbations

Nine renderings are defined in `pilot_contamination.py` (`FORMS`): the original plus
eight perturbations. Six of them form the two measured families — question-level
(`rename`, `paraphrase`, `table`) and evidence-level (`perm`, `rrename`, `both`) —
while `swap` (row reversal, reported on its own) and `distractor` (difficulty
control, excluded from both family statistics) are single forms.

Every form is checked on disk by `validate_forms.py`: the multiset of (label, value)
facts is preserved in all items, the answer never appears as a numeric token, and all
nine renderings are pairwise distinct.

## Easy-suite generator note

`gen_v3.py` was written to write the easy suite to `experiment/data/v3/`. If you run
it, keep that output path; `gen` in `pilot_contamination.py` writes the hard suite to
`experiment/data/canary_items.json`, and the two must not overwrite each other.
